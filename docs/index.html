Political Scientist · Research Fellow at the University of Vienna

Koichi Osamura

I study coalition politics, legislative behavior, and party competition, focusing on presidential systems in Latin America and parliamentary democracies in Western Europe.

I am a research fellow at the Department of Government, University of Vienna, where I obtained my doctorate in Political Science. Besides my substantive work, I enjoy learning new methods and techniques, especially new visualization tools.

Research

What I work on

Coalition politics

How presidents build and keep legislative support in multiparty systems, and what motivates parties and legislators to join and support a coalition.

Legislative behavior

Party cohesion, deviation, and roll-call voting, with a focus on South American legislatures.

Portfolio design

How governments create, merge, and reallocate ministerial portfolios in European democracies.

Methods

Analysis of electoral outcomes, expert surveys, bureaucratic appointments; Text analysis.
